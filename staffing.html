QUESTIONS

What US owners usually ask.

Why not hire someone directly on a job board?

You can, and you would then do the recruiting, training, checking and replacing yourself. With us, one monthly price covers all of that, plus AI training and our own team supervising quality every day.

Will they really work my hours?

Yes. Your assistant works your business day in your time zone, Eastern, Central, Mountain or Pacific, so they are online when you and your customers are.

Where do they work from?

From our office in Cebu, Philippines, or from a set-up home office, under our supervision. Every assistant works in English, and you meet them on video before they start.

What tools can they use?

The ones you already use: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, QuickBooks or Xero, your CRM, help desk, and project tools. If a tool is new to them, we train them on it.

How is my data kept safe?

Your assistant works only inside the accounts you give them, with their own login, so you can see and remove access at any time. We agree how your data is handled before work starts.

What if it is not working out?

Tell us. We first fix it with coaching and clearer instructions, and if the fit still is not right, we find you a replacement.
